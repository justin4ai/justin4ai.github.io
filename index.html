About Me

I'm a first-year M.S./Ph.D. integrated student at KAIST Graduate School of AI (GSAI), supervised by Prof. Jaegul Choo. My current research interests include human relighting and 3D physical world.

I received B.E. in Data Science from Hanyang University. While pursuing the degree, I worked as an AI researcher on 2D face swapping, and as a research intern at Samsung Electronics AI Center.
